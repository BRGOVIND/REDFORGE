Privacy Policy

Last updated · October 2026

RedForge is a local-first application. The planned 2.1.0 desktop release defaults to strict local mode. A separate, explicitly configured connected deployment retains hosted-provider support; this page describes both modes.

What the application collects

RedForge does not require a RedForge account or send prompts and results to a RedForge cloud service. With a loopback runtime, RedForge sends inference requests to a service on your computer. Strict mode blocks hosted-provider requests. In a separately configured connected deployment, hosted requests leave your machine and are handled under that provider's policies.

Where your data lives

Workspace records, including evaluation sessions, findings, and reports, are stored in a local SQLite database on your machine. In connected deployments, requests sent to a hosted provider are also processed by that provider. Review its privacy policy before sending sensitive material.

Optional trace capture

Project trace capture records operational data locally. Prompt bodies, response bodies, custom metadata, and detailed errors are off by default. You can change capture, sampling, and retention settings for each project. Enabling body capture may store personal or confidential information on your device. Redaction is best-effort and cannot guarantee detection of every sensitive value. Keep the local service private, use only data you are allowed to process, and review or delete local records when no longer needed.

This website

This marketing website is a static site. It sets no tracking cookies and runs no analytics. It loads fonts from Google Fonts and requests release information from GitHub so download links can point to current installers. Your browser contacts those services for those requests.

Third parties

Local runtimes such as Ollama run on your machine. Downloading models outside RedForge contacts the selected model source. The strict desktop profile blocks in-app downloads and update checks. In a separate connected deployment, configured hosted-provider calls and explicit downloads contact those services.
